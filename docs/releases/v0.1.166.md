# rLogs v0.1.166

Private regression-verification build. This is not a published GitHub release.

## Live scene lifecycle

- Ends the departed dungeon scene epoch on an authoritative exit or failure across the combat overlay, Automarkers, and Mechanics Map.
- Prevents line-only updates and delayed native observations from resurrecting a dungeon that already closed.
- Preserves a real Asterleeds world transition whether it arrives immediately before or after the dungeon terminal event, then permits the next dungeon to establish fresh scene authority.

## Overlay difficulty

- Carries the exact run difficulty family and range-validated Master tier into the live overlay.
- Renders titles such as `Chaotic - Tina's Mindrealm · Master 20` and redraws when difficulty arrives after the scene name.
- Keeps the tier explicitly unresolved when the packet did not supply it; no difficulty is guessed.

## Automarkers

- Retains the private Marker 1 placement canary from v0.1.165.
- Keeps deliberate sequential re-arming available after a completed or failed placement while preventing overlapping attempts.
