rLogs v0.1.74 makes Combat Overlay health visible alongside parser diagnostics.

- Separates an intentionally disabled, out-of-combat-hidden, or focus-hidden overlay from a failed renderer.
- Reports renderer heartbeat age, consecutive live-feed failures, automatic recovery count, and the latest recovery time.
- Keeps the renderer watchdog and v0.1.73 bounded-feed safeguards active in production without requiring Developer mode.

Open Settings → General → Parser health to compare packet capture, decode, sealing, queue saturation, and Combat Overlay status in one place.
