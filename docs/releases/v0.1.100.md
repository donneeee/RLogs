rLogs v0.1.100 introduces the shared, screen-sized Overlay Canvas and improves automatic-upload failure safety.

- Adds a transparent, always-on-top Overlay Canvas sized to the display, with a high-DPI canvas backing surface.
- Makes Mechanics Map the first movable and resizable canvas module, retaining free zoom, pan, rotation, monster filtering, local game-map assets, and packet-backed mechanic layers.
- Persists the map module layout and supports a locked, click-through play mode; reopening it from Mechanics Map restores editing.
- Shows the canvas without activating it, so opening or restoring the overlay does not take keyboard/controller focus from the game.
- Adds an automatic-upload capability preflight. When Cloudflare artifact storage or hosted verification is unavailable, verified parses remain in the crash-safe local queue and retry automatically with an explicit diagnostic.
- Deploys the gated Cloudflare upload routes while keeping them fail-closed until R2, hosted verification, and the operator promotion flag are all configured.
