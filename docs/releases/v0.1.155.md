## Known limitations

- Other players' complete skill timelines require their own synchronized POV logs. Inbound party skill notification routes remain unpromoted until a controlled multi-client capture proves their current-build route and caster correlation.
- Exact live validation is still required on the gaming PC for world and line changes under its actual network route.
- ExitLag compatibility should be enabled only when ExitLag is in use. Restart monitoring after changing the option.
- Mechanics Map content remains exact-scene and exact-authority gated. A newly recognized scene can clear stale map content without inventing a map or mechanic pack.
