# rLogs v0.1.70

- Aligns live `aDPS` with Resonance's ordinary player-damage clock: it begins on the first accepted player-owned damage event, freezes while no further player damage arrives, and includes an intervening mechanic gap only if damage later resumes.
- Keeps `eDPS` on a paused combat clock and snapshots the completed mobbing totals and clocks at boss entry. A retry restores damage, healing, threat, rDPS, and timing to that exact baseline without deleting the failed pull from history.
- Uses the reviewed local-player dungeon recovery effect as the authoritative retry boundary. The regression guard rejects the same effect on another actor or outside an active dungeon attempt; cooldown restoration, boss despawn/HP reset, and objective reset remain corroborating diagnostics rather than heuristic boundaries.
- Retains the game-owned Mistveil objective gate and authoritative monster-tier chain: mob points complete the gate, arm the boss phase, and the first damage to a native Boss-tier entity opens the boss segment.
- Publishes the reducer-owned attempt-damage and encounter-combat clocks to the Combat Meter and native overlay, so cycling a visible wall-style timer cannot make semantic `aDPS` or `eDPS` count down incorrectly.
