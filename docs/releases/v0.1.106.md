rLogs v0.1.106 makes automatic parse upload recovery durable without weakening consent or live parser performance.

- Persists a small recovery authorization immediately after an opted-in run is proven complete, before the optional privacy export and queue work begin.
- Rebuilds and queues an interrupted submission after restart only when that exact run was authorized at capture time; older history is never swept into uploads retroactively.
- Recovers eligible runs made by older releases only when the sealed log is newer than the still-current saved uploader opt-in, providing a conservative migration path for already-missed submissions.
- Re-verifies the sealed log and privacy-filtered artifact before recovery, preserving the captured region, client build, completion evidence, and original visibility choice.
- Processes at most one recovery while capture is idle and only after ordinary queued uploads are drained, preventing disk-heavy replay work from competing with live packet capture.
- Shows recovered-run and recovery-audit failure counts in Log Uploader diagnostics so a silent postprocessor interruption is visible.
