- Removes the completed Mechanics Map tab from the Developer mode boundary while leaving unfinished tools gated.

- Keeps unsupported builds, missing containers, compiler failures, and asset mismatches visible and retryable without substituting another build's data.
- Retains unrestricted wheel zoom, drag panning, the redistributable coordinate fallback, and all six reviewed encounter families from v0.1.98.
