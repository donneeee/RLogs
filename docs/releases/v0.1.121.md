# rLogs v0.1.121

## Packet-gated three-minute dummy mode

- Add a Dummy control directly to the Combat Meter overlay and to existing editable overlay layouts.
- Arm an exact 180-second test from the first positive hit against the packet-identified elite Guild Hall dummies.
- Lock the test to one attributed player and one target, excluding damage at or after the deadline.
- Invalidate tests when another player deals damage or applies an external player effect to the participant or dummy; self-kit effects remain valid.
- Surface armed, running, completed, and invalid states directly on the overlay control.
