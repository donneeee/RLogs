# rLogs v0.1.113

This release advances the packet-backed custom HUD on the native Overlay Canvas.

- The local-player frame now displays current-build game-classified status/buff
  icons observed on the player.
- Status entries preserve packet-provided stacks, source attribution, and
  duration. Timed entries count down locally and disappear at expiry.
- Names and icons come from the bundled exact-ID presentation catalog and local
  game assets. Unknown effects and effects classified by the debuff atlas remain
  hidden instead of being guessed into the player HUD.
- The Mechanics Map contract is versioned to schema 10 so incompatible older UI
  payloads fail closed.

The v0.1.112 source routing fix remains included: Global deployment characters
on Asteria resolve to North America/Asteria and Bahamar resolves to
Europe/Bahamar at packet ingestion, profile publication, and public parse reads.
