rLogs v0.1.82 adds the first packet-backed Mechanics Map milestone.

- Replaces the Mechanics Map navigation mockup with a live player-relative radar fed by canonical packet events.
- Shows exact local, party, boss, monster, pet, player, NPC, and map-marker positions within the game's current-build 140-unit minimap radius.
- Retains packet-observed facing, death, scene, map, targeted cast, mechanic-status, party, and data-gap evidence while bounding every live collection.
- Clears scene-scoped map state on an exact scene transition and visibly marks positions stale after five seconds.
- Enables current-build effect watches for the audited Cursed Tomb, Void Towering Ruin, Season 3 raid, Coral Sea, Tina, and Wasteland scene families without inventing safe-area geometry.
- Adds a read-only local compiler for the game's official `dungeon_map_bg` texture. Extracted bytes and their provenance catalog stay on the player's PC and are never submitted or shared.
- Falls back to a redistributable rLogs radar theme when the official local texture is unavailable or belongs to another build.
- Updates the bundled Overlay plug-in to v0.1.3.
